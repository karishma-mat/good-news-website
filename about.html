Who We Are

We're a group of singers dedicated to sharing the love of Christ through the musical talents God has given us. Our members are part of all different Christian campus ministries and local churches on the University of Michigan campus and the surrounding Ann Arbor area.

Our talented members arrange, mash-up, and choreograph the songs we perform each semester at our final concerts.

Interested in joining? Our rehearsals are on Sunday afternoons and Tuesday evenings. We hold auditions at the beginning of each semester.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join Good News?

Anyone who’s a current student at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor, M is eligible to join. As this is a Christian a cappella group, we encourage Christian students to join and ask for a brief testimony during auditions.

When do you hold auditions?

Auditions occur at the beginning of each semester. You can find more information at our Instagram or on our Auditions page. More specific questions? Fill out our contact form, and we’ll be in touch!

Do I need to know music theory to join?

Yes, you will likely need a base knowledge of some music theory to join Good News; however, our members have a broad range of experience with reading sheet music. For reference, we typically learn new music in a choir-style format, with time spent in sections to learn parts initially before singing our parts together and refining details as needed.

What grade do I have to be to join?

You can be any grade to be involved in Good News! We accept new members from first-semester freshmen to last-semester seniors.

About Us

We are located at the University of Michigan’s Ann Arbor campus. We host concerts at the end of each semester and monthly worship nights.
