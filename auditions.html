Interested in Joining?

We’re always looking for new members!

Auditions are held at the beginning of each semester. Our audition process involves three stages: initial auditions, callbacks, and results. Click below to find out more about each stage:

Initial audition

For your initial audition, you’ll be asked to prepare an excerpt of a song you like so we can assess your singing abilities. From there, we will give you a short passage to sightread, ask some questions, and give you the chance to ask us some as well.

Callbacks

If you receive a callback, you’ll be emailed after your initial audition informing you of the callback date and time. We’ll also give you the sheet music to a piece we’re working on for you to learn ahead of time. Your callback will simulate a typical rehearsal layout; you’ll meet the rest of the members, participate in sectionals, and sing with the group. We’ll close out the callback with a fun game to get to know you.

Results

After callbacks, you’ll receive an offer to join Good News by the next day if you were selected to join.
