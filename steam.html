Servant Team

Affectionally nicknamed “Steam”, our Servant Team functions to keep Good News running and provide Newsies with fun ways to grow each year.

Sydney Law

Bringing a wide range of musical abilities to her new role, Sydney is studying under Braden’s careful guidance to become our new Music Director, effective Winter 2027. She is a sophomore studying pharmacy and involved in New Life Church’s worship team as well as being the electric guitarist for the band Wisdom Drive.

Julie McKee

A mix between a sophomore and a senior at the University of Michigan, Julie is the resident New Yorker of Good News. Stepping into the role of Inreach Chair, she will lead the group’s fellowship time and bonding events. Julie is a triple threat whose skills involve singing, dancing, and legal counsel.

Braden Vogel

With a tenure of over 2 and a half years, Braden has served as the Music Director for Good News while completing his studies as a student in the Ross School of Business. Now a senior, he is also involved in Salt Company in their worship team and the University of Michigan’s BYX (Brothers Under Christ).

Karishma Mathew

From providing technical equipment to arranging music to writing rehearsal logs, Karishma’s dedication to Good News is unmatched. Now, she is bringing her skills to the role of Business Chair. Aside from her responsibilities in Good News, she is a sophomore studying computer science and linguistics.

Ariana Gustafson

Known as the Good News choreographer, Ariana is widening her scope to include Outreach Chair, coordinating with other groups to bring Good News to different places and events in the community. Besides Good News, Ariana is a double-major in SMTD as a dance major and a harpist. She also attends Salt Company and Treeline Church.

About Us

We are located at the University of Michigan’s Ann Arbor campus. We host concerts at the end of each semester and monthly worship nights.
